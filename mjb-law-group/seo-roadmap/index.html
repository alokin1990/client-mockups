Clear ownership and next steps for every call

  • Connect existing intake records with reporting; keep repeat callers on their current record.
  • Confirm case fit and a specific callback or consultation, with consistent explanations of scope and consultation terms.
  • Agree a proposed missed-call return goal of within one business hour, with an owner, backup and coverage arrangements.
  • Route stated deadlines through the attorney-approved escalation process.
  • Each week, review a small call sample for listening, clear explanations and updates; check response and next-step coverage, qualifications, consultations and accepted matters.

Keep client feedback current

Invite honest, optional feedback consistently after an agreed genuine service milestone. Use the same invitation for clients with a genuine experience; avoid selecting only those likely to leave positive feedback and offer no incentives.

Suggested invitation: “Thank you for working with MJB Law Group. If you’d like, please share an honest Google review in your own words. It’s entirely optional.”

One reminder after 5–7 days: “If you haven’t had time, here’s the link again. Your honest feedback would mean a lot to us. Thank you.”

Public replies: thank people for feedback, keep private case details out and move sensitive discussions to a private channel. The coordinator logs invitations, reminders and replies; monthly reviews track recent feedback without promising a rating or volume.

How we will measure progress

Visibility and engagement

  • Priority employment and wrongful-termination search visibility.
  • Engagement with core service and location pages.
  • Relevant AI-search references and website referrals.
  • Current client feedback, profile activity and approved social-content engagement.

Consultations and growth

  • Employment enquiries aligned with the practice’s target matters.
  • Consultations and accepted matters, reported separately.
  • Callback response, qualification and next-step coverage, supported by weekly intake reviews.
  • Progress on published enhancements and the next local opportunity.

Monthly reviews combine comparable search periods, consultation insights and delivered milestones. Track organic calls separately from Google Business Profile call-button actions; neither is a count of unique clients or accepted matters. Separate article and service-page trends and label partial periods and data lag. AI-search references are tracked separately; their effect on clicks is unmeasured. Review feedback invitations, recent reviews and approved social activity alongside outcomes. The final review informs the next-quarter growth plan.
