Coverage & assumptions

Excluded records

This is the covered-trade scenario, not the full actual Bravos account. Missing trades and stale marks can affect the trend and later allocation sizes in either direction. Negative ledger cash indicates implied financing; interest and broker margin restrictions are not modeled. Fees, dividends, taxes, deposits and withdrawals are absent. Report prices are assumed executions; cached closes may need price-basis verification.
