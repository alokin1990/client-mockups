Choose a finish

How much glass should the frost cover?

These diagrams show coverage choices, not a particular film’s opacity. Lighting, viewing distance, and the selected material change the result.

FULL COVERAGE

Full-panel frost

Give the treated pane a consistent obscured finish. It reduces clear views through the covered area, while silhouettes can still appear close to backlit glass.

PARTIAL BAND

Privacy bands

Cover the sightline and keep selected areas clear. A useful starting point for office partitions, doors, or windows where openness still matters.

PATTERN / GRADIENT

Patterned or graduated

Use a design that shifts across the pane. Open portions remain see-through, so review a sample at the viewing distance that matters to you.

CCUSTOM GRAPHIC

Custom graphic frost

Work a logo or other approved artwork into the frosting. Confirm scale, clear areas, and artwork before production.

Practical answers

Questions before installation.

For a final recommendation, describe the glass, lighting, and privacy goal to Campbell.

Ask Campbell a question ↗
Will frosted film give me privacy at night?

It can obscure detail through the treated area, but it is not one-way. Interior lighting may reveal silhouettes, especially when someone is close to the glass. Review the finish under the lighting conditions you care about.

Can I still see outside?

A full frosted area limits a clear view through that part of the pane. Bands or clear sections can preserve chosen views; the right layout depends on where people stand and what they need to see.

Will it work on my glass or existing coating?

Compatibility depends on the glass, coating, and selected film. Share what you know about the pane and ask Campbell to assess suitability before choosing a finish.

Can I use bands, a pattern, or a logo?

Campbell’s current service information describes partial frosting, patterns, gradients, and custom printed designs. Confirm the proposed artwork, placement, and finish for your specific glass.

How is the film cleaned, and can it be removed?

Care and removal depend on the film and adhesive. Ask for the selected film’s written cleaning and removal guidance; avoid abrasive tools or products unless they are approved for it.

What warranty applies?

Ask Campbell for the written terms covering the selected material and installation. Coverage and exclusions can differ by product and project.
