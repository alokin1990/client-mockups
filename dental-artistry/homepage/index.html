Complete dental services

Explore care by category

Find restorative, cosmetic, emergency, family, and oral-surgery care offered by Dental Artistry. Your dentist can help you decide what fits your needs.

Full-arch optionsAll-on-4

We use implants to support a full arch of replacement teeth for suitable patients.

 Missing-tooth careDental implants

We use dental implants to replace missing teeth and support crowns, bridges, or dentures.

 Replacement optionsDentures

Dentures replace several or all missing teeth and can help with eating and speaking.

 Tooth restorationDental crowns and bridges

Crowns cover damaged teeth, while bridges can replace one or more missing teeth.

 Preserving a toothRoot canal

Root canal treatment addresses infection inside a tooth and may help preserve it.

 Removing a toothTooth extraction

We remove a tooth when it cannot be preserved or removal is part of the care plan.

 Jaw and bite concernsTMD/TMJ

We evaluate jaw pain, clicking, and bite concerns to discuss suitable care.
Shape and appearancePorcelain veneers

Porcelain veneers can change the visible shape, size, and color of front teeth.

 Tooth alignmentClear aligners

Removable clear aligners gradually move teeth according to an individual treatment plan.

 Tooth colorTeeth whitening

We offer professional whitening to lighten stains on natural teeth.

 Small repairsDental bonding

Tooth-colored bonding can repair small chips or change the appearance of a tooth.

 Custom restorationsInlays and onlays

Custom inlays and onlays restore damaged areas of a tooth when a filling may not be enough.
Urgent concernsEmergency dentistry

Call about urgent tooth pain or damage so our team can help plan the next step.
CheckupsDental exams and checkups

We check your teeth, gums, and bite and discuss any changes or concerns.

 Cavity careDental fillings

Fillings repair teeth affected by cavities or other small areas of damage.

 Gum healthGum disease treatment

We assess and treat gum inflammation and infection to support your oral health.

 Preventive careTeeth cleaning

Professional cleanings remove plaque and tartar that daily brushing can miss.
Surgical careOral surgery

Our surgical care includes procedures such as tooth removal and implant placement.

 Comfort supportDental sedation

Ask whether sedation is suitable for your procedure and health history.
