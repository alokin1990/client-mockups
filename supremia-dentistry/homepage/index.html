Our office and technology

Advanced technology at our Wake Forest office

Dr. Suh and the team use diagnostic, treatment, cosmetic, and comfort tools according to each patient’s needs. Ask which tools are relevant to your care.

Diagnosis and oral health +
  • TekscanComputerized bite analysis helps assess how teeth contact.
  • DIAGNOdentLaser-based readings support cavity detection alongside a clinical exam.
  • BioPakRecords jaw-muscle and bite information for selected TMJ/TMD evaluations.
  • Oral DNASaliva testing may add information when assessing gum health.
  • MICROLUXTransillumination helps the team examine teeth for possible cracks and decay.
  • Digital X-raysDigital radiographs provide images for diagnosis and treatment planning.
  • CO₂ laserMay be used for selected soft-tissue procedures.
  • Periodontal therapyHygiene tools and techniques support preventive and gum care.
Treatment planning and restorations +
  • Laser dentistryLaser tools may be part of selected dental procedures.
  • Digital photographyClinical photographs help document findings and discuss plans.
  • Digital impressionsDigital scans can support the design of selected restorations.
  • MyoTronicsJaw-function measurements may inform selected bite and TMJ evaluations.

Dr. Suh and the team continue training in dental techniques and care.

Cosmetic options +
  • Porcelain restorationsMetal-free materials are among the choices for selected restorations.
  • Orthodontic toolsClear aligners and other approaches can be discussed after an exam.
  • Whitening systemsOptions can be tailored to a patient’s tooth shade and goals.
  • Laser contouringMay be considered for selected gum-contouring needs.
Comfort during a visit +
  • Sedation therapyAsk the team which sedation options, if any, fit your health and treatment plan.
  • Chairside comfortsAsk about chair padding, blankets, warm towels, and headphones for your visit.

Common questions before you visit

How do I request an appointment?

Request an appointment online or call (919) 556-6200. Tell our team what brings you in.

Where is the office?

Supremia Dentistry is at 1704 South Main Street, Suite 110, Wake Forest, NC 27587. Get directions.

What kinds of dental care can I explore?

Our services include general, cosmetic, implant, emergency, TMJ and sedation dentistry. Browse all services or tell us about your concern.

Are financing resources available?

Supremia Dentistry offers Cherry, CareCredit and LendingClub as financing resources. Approval and terms depend on the provider and your application. Review financing options.

Can I ask about comfort options if I feel nervous?

Yes. Tell our team about your concerns. A dentist can discuss sedation options that may be appropriate for your care.

What should I do about tooth pain?

Call our Wake Forest office about tooth pain or another urgent concern. A dentist can evaluate your symptoms and discuss next steps. Explore emergency dentistry.
