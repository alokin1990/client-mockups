Am I a candidate for dental implants?

We review bone, gum health, medical history, and factors that affect healing, including tobacco use. Some patients need preparatory care. Your consultation also covers alternatives.

Can I have replacement teeth on the day of surgery?

Selected cases may allow temporary teeth at placement. These are different from the final restoration fitted after healing. Ask what your plan allows and what eating restrictions apply.

Will my replacement teeth be removable?

Fixed implant bridges stay in place for daily use. Removable overdentures come out for cleaning. We will explain which design is proposed and how to care for it.

How is comfort managed during treatment?

We discuss anesthetic and sedation options after reviewing your health and treatment needs. Ask about preparation, recovery, and whether you will need someone to take you home.

What can I eat while my implants heal?

Your team will explain the foods to choose, what to avoid, and how long restrictions apply. Temporary teeth and final teeth can have different limits, so follow the instructions for your own stage of care.

What should I know about long-term care?

Implants need daily cleaning and regular dental checks. Gum problems, implant complications, or wear can require treatment. Tell your dentist about discomfort, looseness, or changes in your bite.
