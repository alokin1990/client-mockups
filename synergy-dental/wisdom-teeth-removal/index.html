A decision based on your exam

Do your wisdom teeth need to be removed?

Not always. Dr. Porter considers your symptoms, the tooth's position, the surrounding tissues, and whether the area can be kept healthy. Removal is recommended when the findings support it, not simply because a wisdom tooth is present.

Monitoring may be considered

When the tooth is stable

  • No current symptoms or active concerns are found
  • The area can be kept clean and checked over time
  • Your dentist recommends observation at routine visits

Monitoring still requires follow-up. A recommendation can change if symptoms or findings change.

Removal may be considered

When the tooth is causing concern

  • Pain, inflammation, or repeated problems are present
  • The tooth is impacted or difficult to clean
  • The position may affect nearby teeth or tissues

Only an examination can determine whether wisdom tooth extraction is appropriate in your case.

Know the warning signs

Dry socket can occur when the protective clot at an extraction site is disrupted or does not remain in place. Contact the dental team if pain becomes severe, worsens after it had begun to improve, or comes with an unpleasant taste or odor.

Call promptly for uncontrolled bleeding, increasing swelling, fever, difficulty breathing or swallowing, or any symptom your aftercare instructions identify as urgent.

Common questions

Wisdom teeth removal in Gillette: common questions

These answers cover common concerns. Your examination provides the recommendation for your teeth.

Does everyone need their wisdom teeth removed?+

No. Some wisdom teeth can be monitored when they are stable and not causing concern. Your dentist will consider your symptoms, examination, imaging when needed, and the condition of the surrounding area.

What is an impacted wisdom tooth?+

An impacted wisdom tooth does not fully emerge into a normal position. It may remain beneath the gum or bone, or come in at an angle. An evaluation helps determine whether monitoring or impacted wisdom teeth removal should be considered.

What happens during a wisdom teeth evaluation?+

The visit generally includes a conversation about symptoms and health history, an examination, and imaging when needed. You then review the findings, possible next steps, and what preparation or recovery could involve.

What comfort options are available?+

Comfort planning depends on your health history, needs, and the planned procedure. Ask the team which options may be appropriate for your visit.

How long does recovery take?+

Recovery varies with the number and position of the teeth, the procedure, and individual healing. Your dental team will give you instructions tailored to your treatment and explain when to contact the office.

How can I reduce the risk of dry socket?+

Follow the aftercare instructions you receive, especially guidance about cleaning, rinsing, food, activity, and protecting the extraction site. Contact the office if pain becomes severe or worsens after it had begun to improve.

Will insurance cover wisdom tooth removal?+

Benefits vary by plan and by the treatment recommended. After your evaluation, ask the office for an estimate and help reviewing the information available from your plan.
