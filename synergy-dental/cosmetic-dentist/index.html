Challenges in traditional cosmetic dentistry

Some conventional cosmetic plans may focus mainly on appearance, use materials a patient wants to discuss, or change more tooth structure than expected. A consultation creates room to compare the purpose, materials, limits, and maintenance of each option before deciding what makes sense.

Bio-friendly cosmetic dental techniques

The current Synergy Dental approach combines appearance goals with attention to oral health, materials, and conservative planning. Suitability and the amount of tooth alteration depend on the findings from your evaluation.

Frequently asked questions

Questions to bring to a cosmetic consultation

The details of any plan depend on an evaluation and the option being considered.

What is included in cosmetic dentistry?

Cosmetic dentistry can include treatments intended to change the appearance of teeth and smiles. This page lists whitening, veneers, ceramic crowns and bridges, biomimetic procedures, and related facial-aesthetics options.

How do I know which option to start with?

Start with an evaluation. Your goals, oral health, bite, the condition of each tooth, and the amount of change you want can all shape the options discussed.

Can more than one treatment be part of a plan?

More than one treatment may be discussed when goals overlap. The sequence and suitability are individual, so the consultation comes before a combined plan.

How much does cosmetic dentistry cost?

Cost depends on the treatment selected and the individual plan. The practice can discuss estimates after the evaluation clarifies the appropriate options.
