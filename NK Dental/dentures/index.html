Detailed guidance

Questions to review before scheduling

Which denture designs may be discussed? What affects fit and daily use? How are dentures maintained?

Which denture designs may be discussed?

Complete dentures replace a full arch, while partial dentures replace selected teeth. Some designs are removable and others may use teeth or implants for support. The examination should identify which designs are relevant and currently offered.

What affects fit and daily use?

Supporting tissues, remaining teeth or implants, bite, saliva, muscle control, and the appliance design affect stability. Adaptation with speech and eating can take time, and sore areas or looseness may require adjustment rather than self-repair.

How are dentures maintained?

Review daily cleaning, storage when the appliance is out, oral-tissue care, follow-up, relines, repairs, and replacement. Fit and materials change over time, so avoid assuming that a denture will feel or function the same for every person.

Common questions

What patients often ask before scheduling

These answers are general. Your examination and treatment plan determine what applies to you.

How do complete and partial dentures differ?

Complete dentures replace a full arch; partial dentures replace selected teeth. Designs and support differ, so the examination should identify the relevant options.

Will dentures need adjustments?

Adjustments may be needed as you adapt or as oral tissues change. Ask how sore areas, relines, repairs, and follow-up are handled.

How should dentures be cleaned?

Cleaning and storage depend on the appliance and materials. Follow the written instructions provided for your denture and oral tissues.
