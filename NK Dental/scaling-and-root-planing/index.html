What the gum evaluation should explain

Gum treatment depends on the depth and pattern of inflammation, bone support, buildup below the gums, and how the tissues respond over time.

01

The diagnosis

Measurements, bleeding, recession, buildup, and imaging help distinguish routine prevention from active periodontal treatment.

02

The treatment area

The number of areas and depth below the gumline affect whether care is completed in stages.

03

The maintenance plan

Ongoing periodontal maintenance and home care are part of controlling disease after active treatment.

What happens next

From measurements to maintenance

The exact sequence depends on the examination and the treatment that is appropriate for you.

  1. 01

    Measure and diagnose

    Review gum measurements, buildup, inflammation, tooth support, risk factors, and indicated imaging.

  2. 02

    Treat affected areas

    Remove deposits above and below the gumline according to the diagnosed areas and comfort plan.

  3. 03

    Reevaluate

    Check healing, reinforce home care, and set an appropriate periodontal maintenance schedule.

Detailed guidance

Questions to review before scheduling

Why may scaling and root planing be recommended? What can affect the number of visits? What happens after treatment?

Why may scaling and root planing be recommended?

Scaling and root planing is a nonsurgical periodontal treatment used to remove deposits below the gumline and clean affected root surfaces when the examination supports it. It is different from a routine cleaning and is not a treatment for the inside of a tooth.

What can affect the number of visits?

The areas involved, inflammation, pocket depth, comfort needs, health history, and response to care influence how treatment is divided. Medication or other adjuncts are used only when clinically appropriate and confirmed in the plan.

What happens after treatment?

Follow the written instructions for eating, cleaning, medication, sensitivity, and warning signs. Re-evaluation helps assess bleeding, inflammation, pocket depth, and home care and determines the appropriate periodontal-maintenance schedule or referral.
