Detailed guidance

Questions to review before scheduling

How do fixed braces work? What does daily care involve? What happens after braces are removed?

How do fixed braces work?

Traditional braces use brackets and an archwire to apply controlled forces over time. The examination and appropriate records should define the bite concerns, movement goals, oral-health needs, appliance plan, alternatives, and monitoring responsibilities.

What does daily care involve?

Cleaning around brackets and wires, following food guidance, using elastics or other accessories when prescribed, protecting appliances during sports, and attending monitoring visits are part of treatment. Ask what to do if a bracket or wire becomes loose.

What happens after braces are removed?

Retention is part of the treatment plan because teeth can shift. Confirm the retainer type, wear instructions, follow-up, replacement terms, and what to do if the retainer no longer fits.

Common questions

What patients often ask before scheduling

These answers are general. Your examination and treatment plan determine what applies to you.

How do braces move teeth?

Brackets and an archwire apply controlled forces. The treatment plan and monitoring visits guide how those forces are adjusted over time.

How do I clean around brackets and wires?

Ask for a demonstration of brushing, flossing, and any recommended tools. Good plaque control matters throughout treatment.

What foods or activities may need changes?

The team should explain foods that may damage appliances, sports protection, and what to do if a bracket or wire becomes loose.

How do braces compare with clear aligners?

They differ in visibility, removability, food routines, daily responsibility, cleaning, and the movements they may be used to manage.

What happens after braces are removed?

Retainers and follow-up help manage post-treatment movement. Confirm wear instructions and replacement terms before treatment.
