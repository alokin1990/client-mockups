Detailed guidance

Questions to review before scheduling

Which stains can whitening change? What does not whiten? What should I ask about sensitivity and maintenance?

Which stains can whitening change?

Whitening can change some natural-tooth discoloration, but response varies with stain type, tooth condition, previous treatment, and the selected method. An examination can identify decay, gum concerns, cracks, or internal color changes that need a different plan.

What does not whiten?

Fillings, crowns, bonding, and veneers keep their existing color. Discuss sequencing when visible restorations may need to match afterward. Whitening also does not correct tooth position, shape, or surface defects.

What should I ask about sensitivity and maintenance?

Ask which professional method is currently offered, how it is used, what sensitivity plan applies, and what result is realistic for the stain type. Shade change, visit length, and maintenance vary; avoid guarantees or fixed shade claims.

Treatment-specific guidance

What determines whether whitening will match your goal

Whitening changes some types of tooth discoloration, but existing restorations do not whiten and sensitivity or oral-health concerns may change the plan.

01

Type of discoloration

Surface stain and color within the tooth can respond differently. An examination helps identify concerns that whitening will not correct.

02

Restorations and shade

Fillings, crowns, bonding, and veneers keep their existing color. Discuss sequencing if visible restorations may need to match afterward.

03

Sensitivity and maintenance

Ask about the recommended method, use instructions, sensitivity plan, realistic maintenance, and habits that may affect how long the shade lasts.

Common questions

What patients often ask before scheduling

These answers are general. Your examination and treatment plan determine what applies to you.

Will whitening change the color of fillings or crowns?

No. Existing fillings, crowns, bonding, and veneers keep their current color, which may affect treatment sequencing.

Can whitening cause sensitivity?

Sensitivity can occur. An examination and the recommended product instructions should guide whether whitening is appropriate and how sensitivity is managed.

How long will the color change last?

Maintenance varies with the method, stain type, diet, tobacco use, oral care, and future dental work. Avoid guaranteed shade or duration claims.
