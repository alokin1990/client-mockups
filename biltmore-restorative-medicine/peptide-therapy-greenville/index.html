A NATURAL, RESTORATIVE APPROACH

What are peptides?

Peptides are tiny chains of amino acids. They occur naturally in the body and are present in every living cell. When linked together, they initiate many different cellular processes. Specific amino acids stimulate the production of particular hormones and proteins. If your body is no longer making enough of its own peptides, it will no longer be able to function at optimal levels.

Peptides have actually been used in medicine for decades! Insulin is a peptide and has been available for nearly 100 years! Because your body already has receptors for peptides and produced them at higher levels when you were younger or healthier, their use should not be considered “foreign” or even “medicinal.” Peptide Therapy merely revives optimal peptide levels to where they were when we were at our peak!

Is Peptide Therapy safe?

Side effects are rare and vary by medication and by patient. They may include injection-site reactions such as rash, irritation or skin sensitivity, and other effects depending on what is prescribed. Your provider will review the full risk profile of any prescribed medication with you during consultation and monitor you throughout treatment.

Is Peptide Therapy a permanent solution?

Most peptides are safe to incorporate into your daily routine, and the full effects of treatment should present 3-6 months from the start of peptide therapy treatment. Some peptides do carry a risk of withdrawal symptoms should you stop taking them, but we will help you plan the best combination and dosages of peptides to reach optimal results.

Can I buy any quality peptide products online or over-the-counter?

How can you possibly trust your health and life to a vial of powder or liquid purchased from non-approved sources without regard to evidence of purity or potency, much less sterility? Just one injection from a contaminated product could result in sepsis, meningitis, or other serious infections. While online peptides are “cheap,” you get what you pay for.

How much does Peptide Therapy cost?

Peptide Therapy is highly individualized. As a result, treatment costs will differ from patient to patient based on their goals and anticipated outcomes.
