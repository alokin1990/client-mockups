Why compounding

When a standard medication does not match the prescription

Compounding allows a pharmacist to prepare a medication from a prescriber’s order when an available manufactured product does not meet the requested strength, dosage form, ingredient, flavor, or administration need.

Every request is reviewed for the prescribed formulation, ingredient availability, preparation requirements, and practical next steps. A compounded medication is not selected from this page; the process begins with the prescriber and pharmacist.

Practical questions

Before a compounded prescription moves forward

Call the pharmacy when you need an answer about a specific prescription, current availability, or delivery timing.

Do I need a prescription for a compounded medication?

Compounded prescription medications are prepared from a prescriber’s order. Ask your prescriber to send the prescription or contact Magnolia Pharmacy so a pharmacist can explain the next steps.

Does Magnolia Pharmacy deliver to Houston?

Magnolia Pharmacy compounds prescribed medications at its Magnolia location and delivers within the Greater Houston area. Contact the pharmacy to confirm eligibility and timing for a specific prescription.

How long does a compounded prescription take?

Timing depends on the prescription, formulation, ingredient availability, feasibility review, and delivery or pickup plan. Contact the pharmacy for current timing.

Will insurance cover my compounded prescription?

Coverage and payment vary by prescription and plan. Verify current coverage and payment details directly with Magnolia Pharmacy and your insurer.

How are refills coordinated?

Refill requirements depend on the prescription and prescriber authorization. Contact the pharmacy before you run out so the team can review what is needed.
