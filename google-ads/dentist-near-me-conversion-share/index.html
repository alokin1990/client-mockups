How to read this

  • Rest of account includes other keywords, dynamic/no-keyword activity and other campaign types.
  • Search-only denominator includes all Search conversions, not just keyword-view rows.
  • Shared account totals are counted once and are not allocated to individual clients.
  • Zero total conversions produces an unavailable percentage, not 0%.
  • Conversion attribution may be revised after extraction.

Formula: keyword conversions ÷ account (or Search) conversions × 100. Source: Google Ads read-only API v25, preserved snapshots and Docker reporting tables. Removed and paused historical keywords/campaigns are not excluded.

Average call length = total recorded duration ÷ unique tracked call records. 120+ call percentage = calls lasting at least 120 seconds ÷ tracked calls × 100. No tracked calls gives no average or percentage, not a zero-second average. Google call reporting is not the full WhatConverts paid-call population. Google Ads call reporting fields and scope.
