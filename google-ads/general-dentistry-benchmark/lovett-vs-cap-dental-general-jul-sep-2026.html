Evidence limits

  • Both totals use the identical inclusive July 1–September 30, 2026 window and reconcile to campaign daily exports.
  • Only the named Search campaigns are included; Performance Max is excluded.
  • The revised estimated CPC, monthly volume, 20-week actions and Action CPA are user-supplied planning targets, not observed results or live campaign settings.
  • The recommended daily budget is calculated as revised estimated CPC × Benchmark's 3.74 matched-period clicks/day; it does not guarantee spend, traffic or conversions.
  • Mixed-campaign reports contain all delivery from the selected Search campaign, not only rows manually classified as general dentistry.
  • Bid keywords are advertiser criteria and remain separate from user search terms.
  • Configured location targets are not confirmed user locations.
  • Google Ads conversions are attributed actions, not necessarily unique people, qualified leads, appointments or new patients.
  • DataForSEO CPC is an external market estimate and is not substituted for observed Google Ads CPC.
