Topic 01

Dental Implants

Explore dental implants
Why are dental implants preferred as a tooth replacement method?

They look, feel, and function much like natural teeth. They can help you eat, laugh, and talk without the slipping, sliding, or clicking associated with some replacement methods. Implants can also help maintain the jawbone.

Am I a suitable candidate for implants?

Many people in good general health can be considered for implants. Your dentist will assess your oral health and the quality and amount of jawbone before recommending treatment.

What if I don’t have sufficient bone?

Implant success depends partly on the quality and amount of jawbone. If there is not enough bone, your dentist may discuss a bone graft before implant placement.

What are implants made of?

Dental implants commonly use a bio-inert titanium root and a ceramic crown.

Do I have to take special care of my implants?

Care for implants much like natural teeth: brush and floss daily and keep regular cleaning and checkup visits. Long-term results vary, so your dentist can give you care guidance for your situation.

Topic 02

Same-day Crowns

Explore same-day crowns
What are dental crowns?

Crowns are caps placed over damaged teeth to restore and protect them. They may be made from several materials; porcelain is often chosen for its tooth-like color and texture.

What are same-day crowns?

Same-day crowns are designed, milled, and fitted in one day with digital technology. Traditional crowns may require two or more visits and outside-lab work.

What is CEREC?

CEREC stands for Chairside Economical Restoration of Esthetic Ceramics. It combines CAD/CAM software and 3D digital imaging to design and mill selected restorations in the dental office.

What are the benefits of same-day crowns?

They provide the restorative purpose of a traditional crown while reducing repeat visits for suitable cases. This can save time and avoid a separate temporary-crown period.

Do same-day crowns fit well?

Digital scanning and computer-guided design support a precise fit. Your dentist still checks the restoration and your bite before completing treatment.

Topic 03

Smile Makeover

Explore smile makeovers
What treatments are covered by a smile makeover?

A smile makeover may combine two or more procedures, such as veneers, crowns, bridges, bonding, tooth-colored fillings, whitening, or gum contouring. The combination depends on your needs and goals.

How long will my smile makeover take?

Timing varies with the type and number of procedures and the extent of change. Some plans involve one appointment; others require several visits or are spaced over weeks or months.

Are smile makeovers costly?

Cost varies with the treatments included in your individual plan. The team can review an estimate after an evaluation.

Does insurance cover smile makeovers?

Cosmetic care is not normally covered, although a procedure that also addresses oral health may have benefits. Check with your insurer and review the plan with the practice.

Am I a good candidate for a smile makeover?

A consultation is needed to determine which procedures may fit. Conditions such as gum disease or tooth decay may need to be addressed first.

Topic 04

Teeth Whitening

Explore teeth whitening
Can I benefit from teeth whitening?

If healthy teeth are stained by foods, drinks, tobacco, or medication, professional whitening may help. Discuss the cause of discoloration and your oral health with a dentist first.

Does teeth whitening work on dental restorations?

Whitening is designed for natural enamel and does not lighten fillings, crowns, bridges, bonding, dentures, or veneers. Your dentist can discuss color matching before treatment.

How long can I expect my teeth to remain white after treatment?

Results are not permanent and depend on personal habits and care. Foods, drinks, and tobacco that stain teeth may shorten the result.

Will whitening damage my teeth?

Professional whitening is planned and monitored to reduce avoidable risks. Your dentist can assess your teeth and gums and recommend an appropriate approach.

Does teeth whitening hurt?

Whitening can cause temporary tooth sensitivity or slight gum discomfort. Tell your dentist about sensitivity so they can suggest precautions or adjustments.

Topic 05

Veneers

Explore dental veneers
What are dental veneers?

Veneers are thin, durable shells made from tooth-colored material and bonded to the front surface of visible teeth.

What are veneers made of?

Common materials include porcelain and composite resin. Material choice depends on the treatment plan and your goals.

Can I benefit from veneers?

Veneers may be considered for chipped, worn, gapped, discolored, uneven, or irregularly shaped front teeth. An evaluation determines whether they are suitable.

Can the veneer procedure be reversed?

Traditional veneer preparation is generally not reversible because a thin layer of enamel may be removed to make room for the restoration.

How long do veneers last?

Longevity varies with design, placement, bite, habits, and care. Regular brushing, flossing, and dental visits help protect veneers and the teeth beneath them.
