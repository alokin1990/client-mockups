How the immediate return estimate is calculated

Loading WhatConverts-qualified peer benchmark…

By default, immediate ROAS* is a provisional lead-value-to-spend estimate: modeled Ads conversion actions × peer WC-qualified share × peer imported value per qualified action ÷ modeled ad cost. WhatConverts may send a quote estimate or sales value to Ads; we have not verified which setting each peer uses. Repeated values suggest presets. This is not confirmed patient revenue or actual ROAS.

If you enter immediate revenue per patient and a qualified-lead-to-patient rate in the cohort panel, the immediate box uses those inputs instead of the peer proxy. Inputs stay only in this browser; Google Ads and Docker are unchanged.
