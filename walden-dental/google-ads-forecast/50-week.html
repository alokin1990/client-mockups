Evidence and limits

Conservative and targeted are two conditional scenarios, not calibrated confidence bounds. Campaign labels come from names; peers can count conversion actions differently. Immediate ROAS* uses the saved peer WhatConverts-qualified share and imported value per qualified action unless you enter patient economics. Neither it nor 3-year ROAS is collected Walden revenue. There is no verified Austin demand ceiling or long-term patient-retention history in this model.
