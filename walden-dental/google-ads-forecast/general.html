Loading WhatConverts-qualified peer benchmark…

By default, the ROAS* card is a provisional lead-value-to-spend estimate: modeled Ads conversion actions × peer WC-qualified share × peer imported value per qualified action ÷ modeled ad cost. WhatConverts may send a quote estimate or sales value to Ads; we have not verified which setting each peer uses. Repeated values suggest presets. This is not confirmed patient revenue or actual ROAS.

Optional Walden-specific inputs replace the imported-value proxy: estimated WC-qualified leads × share becoming patients × revenue per new patient ÷ modeled ad cost. Leave both blank to retain the peer proxy. Inputs stay only in this browser; Google Ads and Docker are unchanged.

The targeted level is the previously labeled optimistic sensitivity: it comes from the 75th percentile of whole-client first-12-week conversion rates, and its week-by-week shape follows complete service peer launches. Peer service labels are name-based, conversion actions differ, and neither Austin delivery nor patient quality has been validated. The historical holdout number assesses the original peer model, not targeted outcomes or the added warm-up rule.
